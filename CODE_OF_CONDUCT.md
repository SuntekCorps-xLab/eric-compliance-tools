# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Welcome different viewpoints and levels of experience.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when necessary, and learn from mistakes.
- Protect private information and follow responsible disclosure practices.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, trolling, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers or the SuntekCorps-xLab organization profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful, threatening, or disruptive.

Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy and will explain enforcement decisions to affected participants when appropriate.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when an individual is publicly representing the project or its community.
